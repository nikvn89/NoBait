Brookfield library proposal discussed
Brookfield council discussed a proposal to close the town library on Sundays during its Tuesday meeting. Councillors did not vote to approve the proposal.
The library will keep its current opening hours. A public consultation and a further council vote would be required before any Sunday closure could take effect.
The council spokesperson said no closure order had been issued. The discussion concerned a possible future saving, not a decision already made.
